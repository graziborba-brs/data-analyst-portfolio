## Highlights

- **Marketing spend optimization:** no acquisition source reached ROMI above 1 in the period. Source 1 performed best (ROMI 0.60); source 3 got the largest budget but had the highest CAC and the lowest ROMI (0.15). Recommendation: keep and optimize source 1.
- **A/B test:** group B converted 15.98% better (p = 0.011), and 18.92% better after removing outliers (p = 0.007). Recommendation: stop the test and adopt B.
- **A/A/B test:** no statistically significant change in user behavior from the new font.
- **Chicago taxis:** one company led with about 71% more rides than the second; the Loop had 12.6% more drop-offs than River North.
- **LA market research:** a small café with about 25 seats fits the local market.
